## How it works

The chart formats dates and numbers for a locale. Changing it updates the labels on the scales and the crosshair.

1. The buttons call `setLocale`, which passes the [`localization`](/docs/api/interfaces/ChartOptionsBase#localization) options to [`applyOptions`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#applyoptions).
2. `locale` takes a language tag such as `es-ES`, and `dateFormat` sets the order of day, month, and year for it.

The chart starts with `ja-JP`. The call is highlighted in the code.
