## How it works

An options chart has prices on the horizontal scale instead of dates. The chart here shows the profit of a long straddle: a call and a put at the same strike, bought for a premium of 8 in total.

1. [`createOptionsChart`](/docs/api/functions/createOptionsChart) creates the chart. It takes the same options as `createChart`.
2. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) gives the series its points. On this chart, `time` holds a price, a plain number.
3. A [baseline series](/docs/series-types#baseline) with `baseValue` at `0` draws the profit in green and the loss in red.

`straddlePayoff` only makes sample data. Replace it with your own.

## Tips

- [`localization.precision`](/docs/api/interfaces/PriceChartLocalizationOptions#precision) sets the number of decimals in the labels of the horizontal scale.
- For a horizontal scale that is neither time nor price, see [Custom horizontal scale](./custom-horizontal-scale.mdx).
