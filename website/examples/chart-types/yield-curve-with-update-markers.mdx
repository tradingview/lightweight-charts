## How it works

A [yield curve chart](/docs/chart-types#yield-curve-chart) has durations, in months, on the horizontal scale instead of dates. This one shows two curves and marks each change to the first one.

1. [`createYieldCurveChart`](/docs/api/functions/createYieldCurveChart) creates the chart.
2. Two line series are added with `lineType: 2` for curved lines and `pointMarkersVisible: true` to mark each point.
3. [`createUpDownMarkers`](/docs/api/functions/createUpDownMarkers) is attached to the first series. Its `setData` sets the initial curve.
4. Every five seconds a few points of the first curve change. The markers' `update` applies each change and shows an up or down marker on the point.

## Tips

- Pass the data through the markers' `setData` and `update`, not through the series directly. Otherwise the markers cannot tell what changed.
