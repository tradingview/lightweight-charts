## How it works

A chart can hold any number of series. Here three line series share the right price scale, so their values compare directly.

1. [`addSeries`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#addseries) is called once per series, each with its own color.
2. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) gives each series its data.

## Tips

- When the series have very different value ranges, give each its own scale. See [Two price scales](../price-scales/two-price-scales.mdx).
