## How it works

A candlestick series draws each period as a body between the open and the close, with wicks out to the high and the low.

1. [`addSeries`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#addseries) with `CandlestickSeries` adds the series. The options set the colors for rising and falling candles, for both bodies and wicks.
2. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) gives it the data. Each item has a `time`, `open`, `high`, `low`, and `close`.
3. [`fitContent`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#fitcontent) fits all the data into view.

`generateCandles` only makes sample data. Replace it with your own.

## Tips

- A data item can set its own `color`, `borderColor`, and `wickColor` to highlight a single candle.
- `borderVisible: false` drops the outline, so the body is filled with one color.
- For candles with rounded bodies, use the [Rounded candles series](/plugins/rounded-candles-series) plugin. It takes the same data and colors as the built-in series, so you can swap one for the other.
