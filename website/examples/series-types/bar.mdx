## How it works

A bar series draws each period as a vertical line from the low to the high, with a tick for the open on the left and for the close on the right.

1. [`addSeries`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#addseries) with `BarSeries` adds the series. The options set the colors for rising and falling bars.
2. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) gives it the data. Each item has a `time`, `open`, `high`, `low`, and `close`, the same as for candlesticks.
3. [`fitContent`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#fitcontent) fits all the data into view.

`generateCandles` only makes sample data. Replace it with your own.

## Tips

- `openVisible: false` hides the open tick, for a high-low-close chart.
- `thinBars: false` draws thicker bars when they have room.
