## How it works

A histogram series draws one column per point, from a base value up to the point's value.

1. [`addSeries`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#addseries) with `HistogramSeries` adds the series. `priceFormat: { type: 'volume' }` shortens large numbers on the price scale, as in `1.4M`.
2. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) gives it the data. Each item has a `time` and a `value`, and here also its own `color`, so rising and falling periods differ.
3. [`fitContent`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#fitcontent) fits all the data into view.

`generateVolumes` only makes sample data. Replace it with your own.

## Tips

- `base` sets the value the columns start from. It is `0` by default.
- To draw volume under a price series on the same chart, see [Price and volume](../price-scales/price-and-volume.mdx).
- For a softer look, use the [Pretty histogram series](/plugins/pretty-histogram-series) plugin. It rounds the corners of the columns, sets their width, and can color them by sign or fade them into a second color.
