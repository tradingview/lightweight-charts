## How it works

This tooltip follows the cursor. Like the [floating tooltip](./floating-tooltip.mdx), it is an HTML element over the chart, updated from a [`subscribeCrosshairMove`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribecrosshairmove) handler.

1. The handler hides the tooltip when the cursor is outside the chart or not over data. Otherwise it fills in the price and date.
2. The tooltip is placed at `param.point`, the cursor position, with a margin. Near the right or bottom edge it moves to the other side of the cursor, so it stays inside the chart.

These lines are highlighted in the code.
