## How it works

This legend shows the symbol, the price, and the date on three lines. Like the [simple legend](./legend.mdx), it is an HTML element over the chart, updated from a [`subscribeCrosshairMove`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribecrosshairmove) handler.

1. `updateLegend` takes the data point under the crosshair from [`param.seriesData`](/docs/api/interfaces/MouseEventParams#seriesdata).
2. When the cursor is outside the chart, it shows the last data point instead, from [`dataByIndex`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#databyindex). So the legend is never empty, even before the first mouse move.
