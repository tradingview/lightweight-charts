## How it works

Lightweight Charts™ has no built-in legend. This one is an HTML element over the chart, updated from a [`subscribeCrosshairMove`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribecrosshairmove) handler.

1. A `div` is added to the chart's container, in the top left corner, with a row for the symbol name and price.
2. The handler reads the price under the crosshair from [`param.seriesData`](/docs/api/interfaces/MouseEventParams#seriesdata) and writes it into the row. When the cursor leaves the data, the price is cleared.

## Tips

- The legend's `z-index` keeps it above the chart, and `pointer-events` are left on, so it can hold links or buttons.
