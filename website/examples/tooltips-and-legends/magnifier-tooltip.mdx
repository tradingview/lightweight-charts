## How it works

This tooltip stays at the top of the chart and moves only along the time scale. Like the [floating tooltip](./floating-tooltip.mdx), it is an HTML element over the chart, updated from a [`subscribeCrosshairMove`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribecrosshairmove) handler.

1. The handler hides the tooltip when the cursor is outside the chart or not over data. Otherwise it fills in the price and date.
2. The tooltip is centered on the cursor's `x` and kept between the left price scale and the right edge of the time scale, using the [price scale](/docs/api/interfaces/IPriceScaleApi#width) and [time scale](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#width) widths. Its top is fixed at `0`.

These lines are highlighted in the code.
