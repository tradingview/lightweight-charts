## How it works

On touch screens the crosshair appears after a long press, because a plain swipe scrolls the chart. With scrolling and scaling turned off, the crosshair can follow the finger right away.

1. `handleScroll: false` and `handleScale: false` in the chart options free touch moves from scrolling.
2. A `touchmove` listener on the container converts the touch position to a price with [`coordinateToPrice`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#coordinatetoprice) and to a time with [`coordinateToTime`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#coordinatetotime).
3. [`setCrosshairPosition`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#setcrosshairposition) moves the crosshair there. A `touchend` listener clears it with [`clearCrosshairPosition`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#clearcrosshairposition).

Open the page on a phone, or in a browser's device mode, to try it.
