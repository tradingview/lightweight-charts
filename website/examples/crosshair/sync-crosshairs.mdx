## How it works

Two separate charts are kept in step: scrolling or zooming one moves the other, and the crosshair of one is mirrored on the other.

1. Each chart's [`subscribeVisibleLogicalRangeChange`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#subscribevisiblelogicalrangechange) handler passes the range to the other chart's [`setVisibleLogicalRange`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#setvisiblelogicalrange).
2. Each chart's [`subscribeCrosshairMove`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribecrosshairmove) handler reads the data point under the crosshair.
3. [`setCrosshairPosition`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#setcrosshairposition) puts the other chart's crosshair on the same time. When there is no data point, [`clearCrosshairPosition`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#clearcrosshairposition) removes it.

The upper chart hides its time scale, so the two read as one.

## Tips

- Syncing by logical range keeps the charts aligned bar for bar. It works when both series have a point at the same times.
