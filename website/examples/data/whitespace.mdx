## How it works

A data item with only a `time` is whitespace: it takes a place on the time scale but draws nothing. Use it for periods with no data, such as a trading halt.

1. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) gets a mix of full candles and items with only a `time`. One of them is highlighted in the code.
2. The chart leaves a gap at each whitespace item, while the time scale keeps its date.
