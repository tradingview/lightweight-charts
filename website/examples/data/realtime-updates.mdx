## How it works

The chart starts with historical candles and then receives a new price every 100 ms, as from a live feed.

1. [`setData`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#setdata) sets the initial candles.
2. A timer passes each new price to [`update`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#update). An item with the time of the last candle changes that candle; an item with a later time adds a new one.
3. The **Go to realtime** button calls [`scrollToRealTime`](/docs/api/interfaces/ITimeScaleApi#scrolltorealtime), which brings the latest candle back into view after you scroll away.

`generateData` only makes sample data. In your project the updates come from your data source.

## Tips

- Use `update` for live data. Calling `setData` on every tick redraws the whole series and resets the user's scroll.
