## How it works

A chart can be split into [panes](/docs/panes), stacked areas that share the time scale. Here an area series and a candlestick series each get their own pane.

1. The third argument of [`addSeries`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#addseries) is the pane index. The candlesticks go to pane `1`, which is created for them.
2. [`panes`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#panes) returns the panes. The candlestick pane is moved to the top with [`moveTo`](/docs/api/interfaces/IPaneApi#moveto) and given a height with [`setHeight`](/docs/api/interfaces/IPaneApi#setheight).
3. [`layout.panes`](/docs/api/interfaces/LayoutPanesOptions) in the chart options sets the separator colors and turns off resizing by dragging.

These lines are highlighted in the code.

## Tips

- A series can change panes later with [`moveToPane`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#movetopane). A pane left without series is removed.
- [`removePane`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#removepane) removes a pane together with its series.
