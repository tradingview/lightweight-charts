## How it works

The volume is a histogram series drawn as an overlay in the lower part of the chart, under the price.

1. The volume series gets an empty [`priceScaleId`](/docs/api/interfaces/SeriesOptionsCommon#pricescaleid). That makes it an overlay with its own invisible scale instead of sharing the right one.
2. [`scaleMargins`](/docs/api/interfaces/PriceScaleOptions#scalemargins) on each series' price scale divides the height: the volume takes the bottom 30%, and the price leaves 40% at the bottom free, so they do not overlap.
3. `priceFormat: { type: 'volume' }` shortens large volume numbers in labels.

These lines are highlighted in the code.

## Tips

- To give volume a separate area with its own separator, put it in a pane instead. See [Panes](../panes/multiple-panes.mdx).
