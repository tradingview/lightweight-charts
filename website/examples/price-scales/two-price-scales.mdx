## How it works

A chart has a price scale on each side, but only the right one is visible by default.

1. In the chart options, `visible: true` on both [`leftPriceScale`](/docs/api/interfaces/ChartOptionsBase#leftpricescale) and [`rightPriceScale`](/docs/api/interfaces/ChartOptionsBase#rightpricescale) shows the two scales.
2. A series is attached to the left scale with [`priceScaleId`](/docs/api/interfaces/SeriesOptionsCommon#pricescaleid) set to `'left'`. A series without `priceScaleId` uses the right scale.

These lines are highlighted in the code.

## Tips

- With two series, the crosshair snaps to the data points of the first one. The example sets the [crosshair mode](/docs/api/enumerations/CrosshairMode) to `Normal`, so the crosshair stays under the cursor.
