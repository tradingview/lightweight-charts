## How it works

The chart reports every click with the time of the bar under the cursor. Here a click on a bar adds a marker to it, and a second click removes it.

1. [`subscribeClick`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribeclick) calls the handler on each click. Its [`MouseEventParams`](/docs/api/interfaces/MouseEventParams) carry the `time` of the bar under the cursor.
2. The handler adds the time to a set of marked times, or removes it when the bar is marked already.
3. `updateMarkers` turns the set into markers and passes them to `setMarkers` of the [`createSeriesMarkers`](/docs/api/functions/createSeriesMarkers) plugin.

The click handler is highlighted in the code. `generateBars` only makes sample data. Replace it with your own.

## Tips

- The same parameters have `point`, the click position in pixels, and `seriesData`, the values of every series at that time. Use them to show the details of the clicked bar.
- To tell the price at the click position, pass `param.point.y` to the series' [`coordinateToPrice`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#coordinatetoprice).
- [`subscribeDblClick`](/docs/api/interfaces/IChartApi#subscribedblclick) works the same way for double clicks.
