## How it works

A price line is a horizontal line across the chart at a given price, with an optional label on the price scale.

1. The minimum, maximum, and average prices are computed from the data.
2. [`createPriceLine`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#createpriceline) adds a line for each, with its own color, [line style](/docs/api/enumerations/LineStyle), and title.
3. The series options turn off the built-in last-value line and label, so only the custom lines are shown.

These lines are highlighted in the code.

## Tips

- `createPriceLine` returns the line. Call its `applyOptions` to move it, or pass it to [`removePriceLine`](/docs/api/interfaces/ISeriesApi#removepriceline) to delete it.
- A price line is horizontal. To mark a moment in time instead, such as an event or a trade, use the [Vertical line](/plugins/vertical-line) plugin. It draws a full-height line at a given time, with an optional label on the time axis.
